Gloamtide — Keeper of the Drowned Light: a deep-sea arena defence game. Swim the reef around a drowned lighthouse, hold its beacon through twelve rising tides of abyssal creatures, gather lumens to level up and choose boons, and slay the three-phase Maw of the Deep. Eight creature kinds, five weapon systems, pickups, combos, dashing, synthesized sound and music, and a victory worth three stars. No build step, no network: open it and dive in.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, hold click to fire, dash with Shift or Space, pause with P, mute with M.

Staged screenshot scenes (seeded, deterministic) live behind `?shot=start|play|boss|win&seed=11`, as listed in `shots.json`.
